Gravitational Wave Group, PMO, CAS

Purple Mountain Observatory, Chinese Academy of Sciences

We study gravitational waves from compact binary systems. Our research covers gravitational-wave searches, black hole ringdown analysis, tests of general relativity in the strong-field regime, and compact binary populations.

Research

  • Gravitational-wave searches
  • Ringdown analysis
  • Testing general relativity in the strong-field regime
  • Compact binary populations
